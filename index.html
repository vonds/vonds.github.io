01 About

Developer, systems administrator and trainer, in one person.

Most teams need a developer, a systems administrator and a trainer. I’m all three, so projects move faster with fewer handoffs. Give me a problem, whether it’s an app that doesn’t exist yet, a server that keeps failing or a team that needs to learn Linux, and I’ll own it until it’s solved.

That’s how I’ve built a cybersecurity training platform, a Linux hardening toolkit and web apps for three community organizations, mostly on my own and from start to finish. BLACK SIGNAL, which I founded, trains people for cybersecurity and systems certifications with hands-on Linux labs, in-browser C compilation and offline access for learners with unreliable internet. Sentinel is an open-source toolkit that hardens and audits Linux servers. And for DEEDS, Pass It On and Beyond the Cover, I designed and built a verified-impact app, a map of free digital resources across four Maryland counties, including Baltimore City, and a roving literary archive.

As a Linux Systems Engineer and technical consultant, I build systems that are reliable, secure and easy for the people using them to understand. That approach comes from years in software engineering, production support and teaching technology as a lead instructor. I’m looking to collaborate with security teams, education partners and organizations that care about fundamentals, access, and shipping work that lasts.
