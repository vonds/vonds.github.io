01 About

Developer, systems administrator, and trainer, in one person.

Most teams need a developer, a systems administrator, and a trainer. I’m all three, so projects move faster with fewer handoffs. Bring me a website or app that doesn’t exist yet, a server that keeps failing, or a team that needs to learn Linux, and I’ll own it until it’s solved.

I’ve built websites and apps for more than ten years, at several tech companies and on my own, and spent three of those years teaching adults moving into tech how to program. Today that work includes BLACK SIGNAL, the certification-training platform I founded, Sentinel, an open-source server-hardening toolkit, and tools for community organizations like DEEDS, Pass It On, and Beyond the Cover.

As a Linux Systems Engineer and technical consultant, I build systems that are reliable, secure, and clear to the people who use them. I’m looking to work with security teams, education partners, and organizations that care about access and work that lasts.

04 · The Traveling Archive
Website · Beyond the Cover04 / 06

The Traveling Archive

A roving literary pop-up that remembers every reader.

The problem
A pop-up literary project needed visitors to respond at every stop, and a way to follow readers from one stop to the next.
What I built
A QR code at each host location opens that stop’s page, with a featured book and its own prompt. Responses carry across stops, so returning readers are recognized and winners can be reached.
Where it stands
Live for Beyond the Cover, which brings Black literature, art, and archives to communities across the DMV.
Visit the site
